Familiar situation

A dealership lists a used car as "one owner, no accidents." The buyer has no way to check — so they haggle the price down anyway, just in case, or walk away over doubts alone.

How VIN Hub solves it

The same car's digital passport shows verified service, repair, and ownership records up front. Buyers see the real history before they even ask — no haggling over doubts.

Why a dealership would bother

Building a track record with buyers one deal at a time is slow, and word of mouth doesn't scale — every new buyer has to trust you from zero.

What keeping a passport gets you

Every verified listing and closed sale builds your public dealership profile and rating — so trust compounds across your whole inventory, not just one car at a time.
