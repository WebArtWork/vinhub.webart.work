# VIN Hub

VIN Hub is a marketplace for buying, selling, and renting cars, built around
one core idea: every car gets a **verified digital passport**. Instead of
taking a seller's word for a car's condition and history, buyers and sellers
look at the same record — service visits, repairs, accidents, and ownership
transfers — tracked over the life of the vehicle.

## The problem

Buying a used car today is a leap of faith. The seller knows the car's real
history; the buyer usually doesn't. Accident damage, skipped maintenance, and
past owners are easy to hide, exaggerate, or simply forget. That information
gap is where trust breaks down — and where deals fall through, get
renegotiated, or end in regret.

## What VIN Hub does

- **Digital passport.** Every car has a running history of service, repair,
  accident, and ownership-transfer records, so a buyer can see the same facts
  the seller does.
- **Listings.** Cars go up for sale or for rent, at a dealership or from a
  private seller, with real pricing, mileage, and condition.
- **Discovery.** Buyers browse a swipeable feed of active listings, or search
  and filter by make, model, price, and kind (sale vs. rental), or find
  nearby dealerships on a map.
- **Dealerships and salespeople.** Dealerships have their own profile, sales
  team, and inventory; salespeople have their own profile and track record.
- **Requests.** A buyer or renter can reach out about a listing — to buy, to
  rent, to schedule a test drive, or to propose a trade-in — without leaving
  VIN Hub.
- **Reviews.** Buyers and sellers leave comments and ratings on cars,
  listings, dealerships, and salespeople, building a track record over time.

## Who uses it

- **Buyers and renters** — browse listings, check a car's digital passport,
  and contact the seller or dealership.
- **Private sellers** — list a car for sale, with its history attached.
- **Dealerships and salespeople** — manage an inventory of listings and a
  public profile buyers can review.

## Status

VIN Hub is currently a static, investor-facing demo: realistic content, no
backend, no persistence beyond local state. See [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) for
the current scope and what's explicitly out of scope for this stage.
